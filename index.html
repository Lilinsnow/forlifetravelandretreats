Our Story

Founded on a passion for intentional travel and meaningful connections

For Life Travel & Retreats was born from a deep belief that travel can be transformative for the mind, body, and soul when designed with love, alignment, and purpose.

Our goal is to support retreat leaders and travelers by managing the logistics — so they can focus on the experience. We create spaces where there is no judgment, where diverse groups come together to connect and find joy.

Every journey allows you to learn something new about yourself by unplugging and reconnecting. The outcome is to be a better version of yourself — more connected to yourself, others, and culturally grounded.

— Clara Cortez, Founder

Explore Our Destinations

Discover transformational retreat experiences in beautiful destinations around the world. Each journey is designed to help you reconnect with yourself. Let's explore the right retreat for you.

Bali Reset + Flow

Known as the Island of the Gods, Bali carries a gentle, heart-opening energy that naturally supports reflection, healing, and presence. Lush jungles, serene beaches, and sacred temples create an atmosphere where the nervous system softens and mindfulness becomes effortless—a place that invites you to slow down, breathe deeply, and reconnect with yourself. World-class wellness sanctuaries, nourishing cuisine, and thoughtfully designed spaces allow you to feel both deeply cared for and profoundly inspired, leaving you open-hearted, grounded, and beautifully restored.

Schedule a Call

Costa Rica Wellness + Glow

A lush sanctuary where nature and wellness intertwine, Costa Rica invites presence, reflection, and deep restoration. From misty rainforests and cascading waterfalls to pristine beaches and vibrant wildlife, every landscape supports nervous system reset and grounded renewal. Move through sunrise yoga overlooking tropical vistas, enjoy guided meditation beside flowing rivers, or immerse yourself in mindful forest walks. Boutique eco-lodges, nourishing cuisine, and thoughtfully curated wellness experiences create a seamless balance of comfort and authenticity—leaving you restored, inspired, and deeply connected to yourself and the vibrant energy of this extraordinary destination.

Schedule a Call

Chile Presence + Renewal

From the cultural elegance of Santiago to the vast, otherworldly stillness of the Atacama Desert, Chile invites spaciousness—internally and externally. Atacama's silence, expansive skies, and ancient terrain create an unparalleled environment for mindfulness, yoga, meditation, and sound healing, where the nervous system naturally softens and clarity emerges. Paired with the pristine beauty of the Galápagos Islands, this journey becomes a celebration of nature in its purest form—untouched landscapes, rhythmic ocean energy, and extraordinary wildlife invite deep reflection and heart-centered presence. A retreat experience that feels both grounding and expansive, leaving you restored, inspired, and deeply aligned.
